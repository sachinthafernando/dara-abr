## 2. Does the diagnosis predict training failure?

Spearman ρ between the misalignment M measured before training and the shortfall of plain PPO after training:
**ρ = 0.873** (95% bootstrap interval 0.778–0.919, 22 reward settings).
Settings flagged by DARA (M ≥ 0.15) fell short of the best fixed tier by 93% on
average; unflagged settings by 1.1%.
